# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Initial Release

Omni-Route combines all your Internet connections together on Windows (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and USB tethering).

- **System Balance**: Transparent per-flow traffic balancing of the entire PC across every active network adapter.
- **Multi-Link Accelerator**: High-performance multi-connection download engine splitting streams into parallel chunks with byte-level verification.
- **Dynamic Gauges & Telemetry**: Live per-link speed monitoring and aggregate real-time bandwidth visualization.
- **Resilient Failover**: Automatic seamless link failover and reconnection with zero interruption.
- **Metered Connection Protection**: Configurable data conservation for cellular connections.
- **Windows Integration**: Native tray service, background operation, and clean per-machine installation.
